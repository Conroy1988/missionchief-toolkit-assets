Staff Training

Choose a station type, dispatch centre or search name, then Scan Stations. Expand stations, select individuals and relevant training. Scan Own Academies, Scan Alliance Academies or Scan Combined (own classrooms first) to review available places and empty seats, then Start Training. Plan entries are labelled Player-owned or Alliance-owned. Show In-Training finds lessons for the current station scope. Complete Training spends the displayed Coins immediately and finishes the whole class. Unmatched lessons remain visible without a payment button. Resume checks uncertain enrolments without repeating them.

Building Level-Up

Open Operations → Building Level-Up. Choose a building type and all or specific dispatch centres, then Scan available level-ups. Choose Credits or Coins and a native target for each building. Price buttons purchase immediately; Purchase Selected runs the selected buildings sequentially. Higher prices, changed scope or uncertain payments pause. Resume checks saved payments first. Earlier hospital plans remain accessible for recovery.

Building Expansions

Choose a building type, dispatch centre and extension. Scan the scope, review eligible buildings and confirm purchases in the selected currency. Preview matches reuses the scan. Choose all eligible buildings, a number, or individual rows. Show excluded buildings explains prerequisites and existing construction. Review & resume verifies uncertain requests without buying again.

Replace matching station icons

In Operations, open Copy station icons, then Switch Icon. Choose a building type and dispatch scope, scan custom icons, choose the original and replacement, and preview the matches. Review the selected buildings before confirming. Saved progress supports Pause and Review & resume. Default icons and unreadable images are excluded. An uncertain upload is verified without automatic replay.

Toolkit help

First setup

  1. Open MissionChief UK and sign in.
  2. Disable your Map Command Toolkit userscript and any duplicate Toolkit extension.
  3. Open the extension popup, enable Toolkit, then Apply & reload.
  4. Open Tasks & settings → Settings manager to import an existing safe settings backup.

The extension copies ordinary userscript settings once, then saves separately. Presets are stored per game account and have their own export/import controls.

Home Response Builder

Open Operations → Home Response Builder. Choose a UK city from search or the dropdown, select an area directly on the map, draw a boundary, or use a centre and radius. City boundary is the default. Choose minimum straight-line spacing of 1, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 10 miles and up to 1,000 new buildings. The maximum is a cap, not a target. Preview proposals, existing-building exclusions, mapped water and the estimated Credit cost before building.

The nearest dispatch centre is selected automatically. Named gold map markers and the distance-sorted list let you change it. Acknowledge the assigned centre and distances before starting or resuming. Choose a supported vehicle and optionally a building icon. Scan icons by building type; the catalogue is remembered for this account in this browser until refreshed or site storage is cleared.

Construction, vehicle purchases and optional icon copying run as a saved sequence. Keep the game tab open. Use Pause, Stop or Review & resume. Recovery checks existing buildings and vehicles before retrying uncertain steps. Review stage timings to see where each location spends time. Minimum spacing is not driving distance or guaranteed coverage. Check small waterways and road access visually; staffing and training are not supplied automatically.

Home Response Unit Switcher

Open Operations, choose Unit Switcher, a dispatch centre (All includes unassigned buildings), current vehicle type and replacement type. Select vehicles, check replacements and review permanent removal and purchase costs. Only available-at-station vehicles are eligible. New vehicles use default names and may require crew training. No refund is assumed. A purchase failure after removal can leave a slot empty; Review & resume continues the saved task. An uncertain request is verified without repeating it. Native controls must be available, with the replacement Credit offer verified after removal. When a single-vehicle shop explicitly reports no space and hides purchase links, a catalogue estimate is shown and acknowledged before removal. Higher live prices or unavailable replacements leave the slot awaiting replacement.

Hospital upgrades

In Operations choose Hospital upgrades. Load hospitals, select a target from 1–30 and choose all eligible hospitals or individual rows. Preview selected costs, then Review & upgrade. The displayed estimate is also the maximum authorised total. Increased target prices pause the job. Pause waits for the current request; closing the panel preserves progress. Review & resume verifies uncertain purchases first. Browser Web Locks support is required. No sharing, tax or medical extensions are changed. Live upgrade acceptance remains untested.

Tasks and recovery

A task is running in another tab

Open Tasks & settings → Task centre → Open task tab. Stop it there or wait for it to finish. Keep the owning game tab open while working.

A task stopped, failed or was interrupted

Review completed, skipped and failed counts and the game’s current state. A request already sent may still finish. Correct the cause, then scan again. Do not assume an unverified action failed and repeat it blindly.

Settings are unsaved or changed in another tab

Keep the tab open and export a settings backup from the settings manager. Do not reload until you have preserved the values you need. A conflicting change is not retried automatically. After backing up, reload and review the current settings before importing selected groups.

The toolkit does not appear

Check the popup’s Connection & compatibility section. Disabled: enable and Apply & reload. Duplicate Toolkit: disable the old userscript or second copy. Unsupported page: open MissionChief UK. Startup failure: export diagnostics, then reload once. If it repeats, include the export and your extension/browser versions in a support report.

Discord reports

Open the extension popup → Discord integrations. Save a webhook and channel label there, then Check connection. In-game, prepare a finance report, sweep report or Operational SITREP. Review the report and optional chart in the Discord screen before Confirm & send. Reports never post automatically after a sweep. If delivery is uncertain, check Discord before preparing another report. A draft expires after 30 minutes. Use Forget destination to remove the saved webhook and draft.

Update safely

Store installation: your browser manages extension updates. An older store version can remain installed while a new submission is under review.

Chrome/Brave unpacked: export a settings backup, replace the files in the same extension folder with the new ZIP contents, reload that existing extension entry, then reload MissionChief.

Orion iOS: use the release ZIP directly with Orion’s file-install option. Prefer updating the existing entry. Export settings and presets before any removal/reinstallation, which can remove local extension data.

Keep the last working ZIP. If a candidate breaks startup, disable it and restore the previous package using the existing entry where possible. Settings added in a newer version may not be understood by an older one.

Report a problem

Include extension version, Toolkit version, browser/app version, desktop or iOS, what you did, what you expected and what happened. Export diagnostics from the popup; it excludes settings, account identifiers and game records. Screenshots and settings/preset backups may contain names or game identifiers—review them before sharing.
