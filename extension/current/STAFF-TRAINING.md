# Staff Training — 1.7.8 local test

Operations → Staff Training filters owned stations by station type, dispatch centre and station name. Scan Stations reads rosters and native vehicle markets. Scan Stations sits beside Search Station. Use each station’s Expand / Collapse button to select individual staff. The sticky selection counter shows total selected staff and classrooms required in groups of 10, without a four-room cap; course choices are restricted to the intersection of the selected stations' supported training. Already qualified and currently training staff are excluded from enrolment.

Scan Own Academies reads native course choices and available room options. Allocation uses the smallest available room count that holds the selection, and continues into another academy when needed. Native rooms currently hold 10 staff, with up to 4 available per academy. Partly filled classrooms are allowed; previews show the shortfall. Scan Alliance Academies allocates existing matching open courses, cheapest first. It never creates alliance courses. Scan Combined uses own classrooms first and then existing alliance places for the remaining staff. Each plan entry carries a Player-owned or Alliance-owned label beside its name. Start Training submits the reviewed plan, after checking staff admission, account, station identity, course, room availability and current price again.

Show In-Training scans the current station scope and matches staff to academy lessons. A lesson must be positively matched before a completion button appears. Unmatched staff remain visible. Complete Training displays the native Coin price and directly finishes the entire lesson, including any other participants, without a popup. A durable journal precedes each mutation; uncertain enrolments and Coin completions are verified without replay. Browser locking serializes these actions with other administration tasks.

Native reference inspection was read-only on MissionChief UK: station staff and vehicle markets, own academy forms, admission checkboxes, alliance open-course listings and academy running-course controls. The catalogue records native descriptions from fire, medical, police and SAR academies. Vehicle-based relevance uses those descriptions; mission-only courses have explicit station-type rules. Unreadable or changed native markup fails closed. This is a UK test implementation; future game course changes may require updating the catalogue.

Validation: parser, relevance, allocation, fresh form, admission, recovery and simulated DOM interaction tests. No live staff enrolment or Coin payment was made. Live game acceptance remains pending.
