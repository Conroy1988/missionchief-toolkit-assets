MissionChief Map Command Toolkit 1.0.0

Full extension release · 13 September 2026

Home Response Builder

  • Plan across UK city boundaries, places selected on the map, drawn areas or a centre and radius.
  • Choose 1, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 10-mile minimum spacing and up to 1,000 new locations. Capped plans sample across the area.
  • Preview proposed buildings, nearby existing Home Responses, spacing circles, exclusion counts and estimated Credit costs. Bundled coastline and major-lake data exclude mapped water.
  • Select from 14 verified vehicle options. Preloaded dispatch centres appear nearest first with named map markers; the closest is selected automatically. Confirm the assignment and distances before building.
  • Copy a selected building icon during construction. Scan by building type and reuse a remembered catalogue of distinct icons.
  • Saved sequential construction with balance checks, Pause, Stop and Review & resume. Verified recovery avoids repeating completed building and vehicle purchases.
  • Fewer duplicate requests, concurrent independent reads and per-building stage timings.

Command workspace and existing tools

  • Compact black-and-red interface, individually floating map controls and the official Game Guide beside Menu.
  • Map and building filters, mission insights, native-map tools and configurable shortcuts.
  • Operations for transport sweeps, recruitment, courses, expansions and station icons. Back to Operations remains reachable while preserving work.
  • Enhanced finance views, on-demand historical reports and optional reviewed Discord reports.
  • Settings search, presets, task status, import/export and local diagnostics.

Before running a build

Distances are straight-line spacing, not driving times or guaranteed coverage. Review access and small waterways on the map. Staffing and training are not supplied automatically. Keep the game tab open and use Review & resume after interruptions.

59 automated regression checks passed. Builder acceptance was reported during testing; the latest speed improvement has not been benchmarked on a live account.
